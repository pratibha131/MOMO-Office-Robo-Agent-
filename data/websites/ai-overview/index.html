Foundations

What is Artificial Intelligence?

AI is the field of building systems that perform tasks which normally require human intelligence — recognizing patterns, understanding language, making decisions, and learning from experience.

🧠

Machine Learning

Algorithms that improve automatically by learning patterns from data, rather than being explicitly programmed for every rule.

🔤

Natural Language

Systems that understand, generate and converse in human language — powering chatbots, translators and voice assistants.

👁️

Computer Vision

Teaching machines to interpret images and video — from face unlock to medical scan analysis and self-driving perception.

🕸️

Neural Networks

Layered, brain-inspired architectures that power modern deep learning and today's large language models.

🎯

Reinforcement Learning

Agents that learn by trial and error, optimizing for long-term reward — used in robotics, gaming and resource management.

⚙️

Generative AI

Models that create new text, images, audio and code — the technology behind today's AI assistants and creative tools.

A Brief History

From symbols to superintelligence talk

AI has gone through cycles of hype and "winters" — here are the moments that mattered most.

1956

The Dartmouth Workshop

The term "Artificial Intelligence" is coined, launching AI as a formal field of research.

1997

Deep Blue beats Kasparov

IBM's chess computer defeats the reigning world champion, an early proof that machines could outperform humans at complex reasoning tasks.

2012

Deep Learning breakthrough

Neural networks trained on GPUs dramatically outperform prior methods on image recognition, kicking off the modern AI boom.

2022–Now

The Generative AI era

Large language models bring conversational AI, coding assistants and image generation to hundreds of millions of everyday users.
